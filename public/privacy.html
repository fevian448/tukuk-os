This policy explains what data Tukuk-OS (“the service”) collects, why it is collected, and the rights you have over it. The service is a free web search engine.

1. Summary

  • No account or registration is required.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use advertising tools to build user profiles.
  • We allow Google AdSense ads for financial support.

2. Data collected

2.1 Server logs

When you use the search engine, our servers record the IP address, time, and requested path. These logs are used to detect and block attacks (rate limiting) and to keep the service stable. Logs are not used for advertising.

2.2 Cookies

The search functionality itself does not use cookies for search sessions, and no search history is stored. Third-party tools listed below may set cookies or use similar technologies.

Google AdSense and ad providers may use cookies to measure ad effectiveness. These cookies are controlled by Google, not by us. You can manage them through Google Ad Center or your browser privacy settings.

Microsoft Clarity — we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used (heatmaps, scroll and click patterns, performance metrics). Clarity may set cookies and use local storage or similar technologies. Data is processed by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. You can review or opt out through the Microsoft privacy dashboard or the Microsoft ad settings.

2.3 Indexed content

This service indexes public web pages so that search can work. For each page we store the title, description, up to 6000 characters of text, links, and the URL. We respect robots.txt files and do not index pages that are disallowed.

2.4 Third parties

Third parties that may receive data:

  • Google AdSense — advertising
  • Microsoft Clarity — usage analytics (heatmaps, scroll/click patterns, performance)
  • Cloudflare — content delivery network, TLS certificates, and firewall

3. Purpose of processing

Data is processed to provide search functionality, keep the service stable, protect the system against attacks, and to fund the service through advertising.

4. Retention

  • Server logs: kept for a maximum of 30 days.
  • Indexed documents: kept until the source content changes or upon request from the owner.

5. User rights

You may request:

  • a copy of the data we hold about you;
  • deletion of your log records;
  • removal of your pages from the index — send the URL through the Contact page.

We answer within 14 working days.

6. Third-party copyright

This service indexes third-party pages. The original content remains the copyright of its original owner. For requests, you can contact us and we will remove the URL from the index.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The update date is shown above. Significant changes will be announced on the home page.

8. Contact us

Questions about privacy: contact us.
